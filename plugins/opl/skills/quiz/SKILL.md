---
name: quiz
description: Interview the user. Use when designing / planning.
---

Ask between 3-5 questions at a time, for as long their blast radius is independent. If there's dependency on resolution of the question, queue the question and ask the later ones once the dependency is resolved. In design document/plans try to keep longer spool of questions if applicable, so one can "look ahead" what will be (possibly) asked next.
